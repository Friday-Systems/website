## Cookies Policy

### What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be technical (necessary for the site to work) or serve other purposes such as analytics.

### Cookies we use

| Cookie | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| [session/technical cookies, if any] | Technical | Site operation | Session |
| [_ga, _ga_* or equivalent] | Analytics ([provider]) | Anonymous usage statistics | [e.g. 13 months] |

Technical cookies do not require consent. Analytics cookies are only set after you accept them via the cookie banner.

### Managing cookies

You can accept or reject analytics cookies in the banner shown on your first visit, change your choice at any time via [the "Cookie settings" link in the footer], and delete cookies through your browser settings. Rejecting analytics cookies does not affect your use of the site.
